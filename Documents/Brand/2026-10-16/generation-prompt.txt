DESIGN DECISION — "FOLDED WORLDS":
A single compact, original, sculptural M symbol made from a continuous broad folded band, two stable upright legs and gently rounded outer shoulders with precise diagonal inner folds. Near-frontal view, very shallow dimensionality, no isometric receding side. The negative space opens clearly from the bottom as one inviting doorway; one concise lozenge-shaped aperture is integrated INTO the central fold as the identifying detail, not a separate diamond floating above. The center is elegantly resolved with only two or three large planes; no tiny triangulated shards, no dense knot. Broad balanced silhouette, width modestly greater than height, equal visual weight on both sides. Firm confident geometry blended with warm sculptural shoulders. It should feel like a piece of contemporary graphic design, not a castle, not an electronics badge. Design as a flat-first mark whose shallow rendering is optional. A clear M is the first read and an invitation into worlds the second.
Palette: deep aubergine violet and warm amber/apricot, with a single restrained intermediate orange transition at the fold. TWO color families, no cyan blue neon rainbow, no chrome. Ink navy/charcoal and warm chalk neutral. Main mark is rich smooth satin, almost flat, gentle tonal change only to explain the fold. Avoid expensive-looking gold-metal luxury clichés.
