## Confirmed user brief

- The studio makes cozy, casual and action games; the identity must span genres.
- Persian “مدین گیمز” and English “MEDIAN GAMES” are both required.
- Full creative freedom; preserving the earlier M is not mandatory.
- Review the supplied Arts folder and Desktop/Logos explorations and synthesize one strong proposal.
- Desired character: distinctive, memorable and credible for a game-development company, with a balance of warmth and authority.

## Review and synthesis

Reviewed all 25 supplied images: 8 in Arts and 17 in Desktop/Logos, including their application boards. These are supplied design explorations, not verified brand guidelines. Text inside those images was treated as reference content rather than instructions or confirmed company history.

Retain the portal-M, the visual tension of a folded band and warm/cool color dialogue. Avoid mascot faces and gamepad buttons, which pull toward casual-only branding, and literal ancient ornament, which pulls toward historical fantasy. The user's desired corporate prestige is interpreted as clarity, restraint and confidence rather than decorative complexity.

## Proposed concept — Folded Worlds

A broad folded M frames an open doorway. A small integrated lozenge aperture in the center is the identifying detail. Firm diagonal planes and stable legs provide weight; softened outer shoulders provide warmth. Deep violet meets amber/apricot, supported by ink and warm ivory. The same idea is shown on light and dark backgrounds with one-color silhouette studies.

This proposal is the designer's recommendation, not user-approved identity. The palette, central aperture, proportions and typography remain provisional. The approved website theme and currently published logo are not replaced.
